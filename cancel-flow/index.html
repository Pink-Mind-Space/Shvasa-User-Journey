    Why this screen

    Discount caps (proposed)

    • Maximum 25% ("up to"). Each reason has its own limit below.
    • Cost, one-time offer: up to 25%, only for members with no practice in the last 30 days or more
    • Service, thank-you credits or days: up to 15% of monthly plan
    • Inactive, earn-back days: up to 10% of monthly plan
    • Comeback, after cancelling: up to 20% on first plan
    • Never stacked above 25% in one plan period. Refunds are not discounts.

    Only the 25% is from you. The split is my proposal; tell me what to change.
